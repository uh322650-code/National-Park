🌿 What is a national park?

"A national park is a protected natural area where plants, animals, and nature are protected and preserved."

"Taman nasional adalah kawasan alam yang dilindungi untuk menjaga dan melestarikan tumbuhan, hewan, dan alam."

In simple words: "A national park is a special place where people can enjoy and learn about nature while plants, animals, and the environment are protected."

"Taman nasional adalah tempat khusus di mana orang dapat menikmati dan belajar tentang alam, sementara tumbuhan, hewan, dan lingkungan dilindungi."

🌳 Natural area

A national park is a natural area.

Taman nasional merupakan kawasan alam.

🛡️ Protected area

It is protected to help preserve nature.

Kawasan ini dilindungi untuk menjaga kelestarian alam.

🌿 Plants

Many kinds of plants can grow in a national park.

Berbagai jenis tumbuhan dapat tumbuh di taman nasional.

🦎 Animals

Different animals can live in national parks.

Berbagai jenis hewan dapat hidup di taman nasional.

📚 Learning

People can learn about plants, animals, and nature.

Orang dapat belajar tentang tumbuhan, hewan, dan alam.

🥾 What can we do in a national park?

🚶 Walk

We can walk around the national park.

Kita dapat berjalan-jalan di sekitar taman nasional.

👀 Observe

We can observe plants and animals.

Kita dapat mengamati tumbuhan dan hewan.

📝 Take notes

We can take notes about the plants we see.

Kita dapat mencatat tentang tumbuhan yang kita lihat.

📷 Take photos

We can take photos of beautiful scenery.

Kita dapat mengambil foto pemandangan yang indah.

📚 Learn

We can learn about nature.

Kita dapat belajar tentang alam.

🗺️ Visit areas

We can visit different areas in the national park.

🌱 Be a responsible visitor

Saat mengunjungi taman nasional, pengunjung harus menjaga alam.

DO ✅

  • Keep the park clean.
    Jaga kebersihan taman.
  • Follow the rules.
    Ikuti aturan.
  • Observe animals from a safe distance.
    Amati hewan dari jarak aman.
  • Take photos responsibly.
    Ambil foto secara bertanggung jawab.

DON'T ❌

  • Don't litter.
    Jangan membuang sampah sembarangan.
  • Don't damage plants.
    Jangan merusak tumbuhan.
  • Don't disturb animals.
    Jangan mengganggu hewan.
  • Don't leave trash.
    Jangan meninggalkan sampah.

⏳ SIMPLE PAST TENSE

Talking about past experiences — Going to a National Park

1. What is Simple Past Tense?

"Simple Past Tense is used to talk about actions or events that happened and finished in the past."

"Simple Past Tense digunakan untuk membicarakan tindakan atau kejadian yang terjadi dan sudah selesai di masa lalu."

"I visited a national park yesterday."
"Saya mengunjungi taman nasional kemarin."

2. When do we use Simple Past Tense?

  1. Menyatakan kegiatan yang sudah terjadi di masa lalu.
  2. Menceritakan pengalaman atau perjalanan yang sudah terjadi.
  3. Menceritakan rangkaian kejadian di masa lalu.
"I went to a national park last holiday."
"I saw many trees."
"I took some photos."
"I visited the orchid house."
"I learned about plants."

3. Time expressions

"I visited a national park yesterday."
"We went there last holiday."
"I saw many animals two days ago."

4. ✅ Positive sentence

SUBJECT + VERB 2 + OBJECT/COMPLEMENT

I + visited + a national park. → I visited a national park.

Artinya: Saya mengunjungi taman nasional.

5. Regular verbs

Regular verbs adalah kata kerja yang biasanya membentuk bentuk lampau dengan menambahkan -ed.

Verb 1Verb 2Meaning
visitvisitedmengunjungi
walkwalkedberjalan
observeobservedmengamati
learnlearnedbelajar
labellabeledmemberi label
"I visited the national park."
"We walked around the park."
"I observed the plants."

6. Irregular verbs

Irregular verbs tidak menggunakan pola -ed. Bentuk past-nya harus dihafalkan. Klik kartu untuk melihat perubahannya!

"I saw a Komodo dragon."
"I visited an orchid house."
"I walked in the forest."
"I took some photos."

7. ❌ Negative sentence

SUBJECT + DID NOT / DIDN'T + VERB 1 + OBJECT/COMPLEMENT
"I didn't go to the national park."
"I didn't see any animals."
"I didn't visit the orchid house."
"We didn't take photos."
⚠️ REMEMBER!
❌ I didn't went to the park.
✅ I didn't go to the park.

❌ I didn't saw the animals.
✅ I didn't see the animals.

"After didn't, use Verb 1, not Verb 2."
"Setelah didn't, gunakan Verb 1, bukan Verb 2."

8. ❓ Yes/No questions

DID + SUBJECT + VERB 1 + OBJECT/COMPLEMENT?
"Did you visit a national park?" — Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
"Did you see any animals?" — Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
"Did you take photos?" — Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.

9. 🔎 Wh-questions about past experiences

WH-WORD + DID + SUBJECT + VERB 1 ... ?
Where did you go? → I went to a national park.
When did you go? → I went there last holiday.
How did you go there? → I went there by bus.
What did you see? → I saw many trees and flowers.
What did you do there? → I took photos and observed the plants.

10. ⭐ Remember this!

POSITIVE
I went to the park.
QUESTION
Did you go to the park?
NEGATIVE
I didn't go to the park.

"Verb 2 digunakan pada kalimat positif. Setelah did/didn't, kata kerja kembali ke Verb 1."
